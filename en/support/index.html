Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, encrypted. Unless you turn on cloud sync, none of your health entries leave your device; the partner feature only sends the closeness status you choose to share. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

I'm changing phones; how do I move my entries?

On your old phone, create a backup with Profile → Privacy & Security → Export My Data. On the new phone, restore it from the same screen with Restore from backup. Or turn on cloud sync in Profile → Account & cloud backup, then on the new phone tap "Restore my records from the cloud" on the welcome screen and sign in with the same account.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete all my data. If you paired with a partner, your pairing details and shared status are deleted from the server too, and if you signed in, so are your account and cloud copy. Reinstalling the app does not bring old entries back. If you can't access the app, you can request deletion of your account and cloud copy at niluapp@proton.me.

How do I cancel my subscription?

The store manages subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nilu. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nilu. A cancellation stays active until the end of the period.

My Premium purchase doesn't show.

On the Premium screen, tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

What does partner mode share?

Only the closeness status you choose to share with your explicit consent (desire level and affection needs). Your cycle dates, symptoms and notes are not shared. Turning sharing off deletes your status from the server.

Nilu does not diagnose, does not prescribe treatment and is not a contraceptive method. Predictions are calculated from your past entries and are approximate.
