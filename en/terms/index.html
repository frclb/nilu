4. Free version, ads and Premium

The core features are free: cycle tracking, logging, the calendar, the list of your full cycle history, reminders, the app lock and creating a restorable backup. The free version shows banner ads; ads are not personalised and no health data is sent to the ad network (see the Privacy Policy). A Premium subscription removes ads and unlocks: the cycle trend chart across your whole history, symptom–phase analysis, the summary and table files you can take to a clinician, and custom themes. Pregnancy and postnatal tracking is free too: appointments and their reminders, warning signs, measurement alerts and the on-screen appointment summary. Sharing the appointment summary as a file is part of Premium.

6. Your data and backups

Your health records are stored on your device. If cloud sync is off and you delete the app, lose your device or reset it, those records cannot be recovered. If you turn on the optional cloud sync, you can get your records back on another device with the same account; turning sync off or deleting your account also deletes the cloud copy. Cloud sync is not a backup guarantee, so exporting a copy is still recommended. Exporting and keeping a copy of your data is your responsibility; creating a backup is free. Restoring a backup does not delete anything on your device — only missing entries are added. If you protect a backup with a password, the password isn't stored anywhere; if you forget it, the backup cannot be opened.
