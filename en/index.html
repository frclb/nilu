Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, encrypted. Unless you turn on cloud sync, none of your health entries leave your device; the partner feature only sends the closeness status you choose to share. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

I'm changing phones; how do I move my entries?

On your old phone, create a backup with Profile → Privacy & Security → Export My Data. On the new phone, restore it from the same screen with Restore from backup. Or turn on cloud sync in Profile → Account & cloud backup, then on the new phone tap "Restore my records from the cloud" on the welcome screen and sign in with the same account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

The store manages subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nilu. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nilu. A cancellation stays active until the end of the period.
