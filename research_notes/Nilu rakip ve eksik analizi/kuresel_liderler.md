## 1. What a free user gets, what is paywalled, prices (USD / TRY / EUR), trials, ads, business model, and 2024–2026 changes

### Takeaway
Flo and Clue are freemium subscription businesses (no evidence of in-app banner ads) whose free tiers cover basic period/ovulation logging and predictions, while insights, expert content, partner sharing (Flo for Partners / Clue Connect) and the specialty modes are increasingly paywalled; Stardust is freemium with an astrology twist and a 7-day/1-month trial SKU structure; Period Calendar (Simple Design) is a free, ad-supported Android app (no iOS listing found); Apple Cycle Tracking is entirely free, ad-free and built into iOS. In Turkey, prices are far lower nominally (e.g., Clue ₺64,99/month vs $9.99 in the US) and the strongest iOS chart performer in the category is not a global brand but an ad-supported generic calendar app.

### Inferences
- Price-point mapping (inference, not confirmed by Apple's pages): Flo's highest US Premium SKU ($59.99; ₺639,99; ~€39,99) is likely the annual plan and the $1.99/$7.49 SKUs are likely weekly/intro offers, consistent with Flo's help center mentioning weekly/monthly/annual intervals. Clue's labeled SKUs make it clear: $9.99/month and $39.99/year in the US; €9,99/month and €29,99–€39,99/year in Germany; ₺64,99–₺189,99/month and ₺259,99–₺379,00/year in Turkey.
- Turkey uses heavily discounted regional price tiers versus USD/EUR for all three subscription apps; the local chart leader charges ₺39,99/month and sells ad removal (₺149,99), signalling a price-sensitive market where free + ads and cheap tiers compete with global premium brands.
- Partner sharing is paywalled at both Flo (Flo for Partners in Premium) and Clue (Clue Connect in Clue Plus), whereas Stardust advertises friend/partner sync in its main description (free vs paid status unclear) and Apple offers none — partner sharing that is free could be a differentiator.
- Apple's iOS 27 perimenopause features commoditize what Flo ("Perimenopause Score") and Clue (Perimenopause mode in Plus) sell, at least for iPhone users who accept basic functionality.
- No source showed in-app display ads in Flo, Clue or Stardust; their monetization is subscriptions (plus, per App Store privacy labels, marketing/attribution tracking). Period Calendar and the TR #4 app monetize with ads + IAP.

## 2. Accounts, data storage, encryption, anonymous modes, privacy incidents, regulatory actions and court cases, third-party sharing, GDPR

### Takeaway
Apple's Cycle Tracking has the strongest privacy architecture (on-device encryption; end-to-end encrypted iCloud sync with 2FA). Flo carries the heaviest legal baggage (2021 FTC order; 2025 class-action settlements of $59.5M with Flo/Google/Flurry; an August 2025 jury verdict against Meta under California's CIPA with potential damages up to billions, still unresolved in Meta's mid-2026 filing) but has since built the most sophisticated privacy feature (OHTTP-based Anonymous Mode, ISO 27001/27701). Clue leans on German/EU GDPR protections for all users. Stardust has twice been caught contradicting its privacy marketing (2022 encryption claims; July 2026 Mozilla finding of health data sent to RudderStack). Period Calendar is an ad-tech-heavy app flagged by Mozilla and Privacy International.
